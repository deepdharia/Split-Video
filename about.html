About SplitVideo

SplitVideo is a free tool for splitting a video into equal clips — by length or by number of parts — without uploading it anywhere. It was built to solve one specific, annoying problem: turning a long recording into pieces short enough for Reels, Shorts, or WhatsApp Status, without installing an app or handing your file to a server.

It's built and maintained by Deep Dharia, an independent developer based in Delhi, India.
