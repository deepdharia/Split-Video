How to split a long video into Instagram Reels

Instagram Reels caps out at 90 seconds. If you've got a longer recording — a talk, a vlog, a tutorial — the fastest way to turn it into multiple Reels is to split it into equal chunks first, then post them as a series.

The quick way

You don't need Premiere or CapCut for this if all you want is equal-length cuts with no editing. SplitVideo does this in three steps, entirely in your browser:

  1. Upload your video (drag and drop, or tap to choose one).
  2. Pick a clip length — 15s, 30s, 60s, or 90s presets match Reels exactly.
  3. Tap Split video. Each clip downloads to your device automatically as it's ready.

Why not just use a phone app?

Phone apps work, but most add a watermark on the free tier, or lock longer clip lengths behind a subscription. A browser tool skips the install and the paywall — and since nothing uploads to a server, it works even on a patchy connection.

Tips for better Reels from split clips

  • Split at 90 seconds if your source video has natural pauses near that mark — you'll get cleaner cuts than a mid-sentence chop.
  • Post split clips as a numbered series ("Part 1/4") so viewers know to expect more.
  • Vertical source footage (9:16) will look best — Reels crops horizontal video automatically, which can cut off important parts of the frame.
