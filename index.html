How it works

SplitVideo runs entirely in your browser using WebAssembly — the same technology that lets apps like Figma run without installing anything. Your video is never uploaded to a server, which means it's private by default and works even on a slow connection, since only the tool itself loads from the internet, not your file.

Frequently asked

Does my video get uploaded anywhere?
No. Splitting happens on your device. Nothing leaves your browser.
Is there a watermark?
No watermark, no login required.
What file size can I split?
Up to 500MB reliably. Larger files risk crashing the browser tab — we're working on a way to handle bigger uploads.
Will this work on my phone?
Yes — it's built mobile-first. Performance depends on your phone's memory for larger files.
