Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 September 2026

Your videos

SplitVideo processes video files entirely within your browser using WebAssembly. Your video files are never uploaded to, or stored on, our servers.

Information we collect

We use privacy-friendly web analytics (Cloudflare Web Analytics) to understand aggregate traffic — pages visited, approximate location by country, device type. This does not use cookies and does not track you individually.

If you use our contact form, we collect the name, email, and message you choose to provide, solely to respond to you.

Advertising

This site may display ads served by Google AdSense. Google may use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this or other websites. You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.

Third-party services

The video-splitting engine (ffmpeg.wasm) and fonts are loaded from third-party content delivery networks (unpkg.com, Google Fonts). These services may log standard request data such as your IP address as part of normal web delivery.

Contact

SplitVideo is operated by Deep Dharia, based in Delhi, India. Questions about this policy can be sent to build@splitvideo.in.
